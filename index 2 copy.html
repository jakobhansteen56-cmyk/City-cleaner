How City-cleaner works

City-cleaner lets you report areas that need cleaning. You send a photo and the address, and we check that the report is valid before saving it.

Step 1 – Upload a photo

Take or choose a photo of the area that is not clean. The image must show an outdoor street or similar public space (not indoors). The area should clearly need cleaning (litter rating at least 2).

Step 2 – Enter the address

Enter the address where the photo was taken. The address must be in Karlsruhe. We use this to forward the report to the right place.

What happens next?

Our system checks that the image shows an outdoor street, that the area needs cleaning, and that the address is in Karlsruhe. If everything is OK, your report is saved. If not, you will see a short message explaining why (e.g. image indoors, area too clean, or address outside Karlsruhe).
